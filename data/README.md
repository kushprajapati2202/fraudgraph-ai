# Synthetic dataset (ds-v1)

Every record here is **fictional**. It was generated by `backend/services/synthetic_data.py` with seed `2026` and
contains no real account numbers, names, phone numbers or customer data.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `accounts.csv` | `account_id, account_type, created_at, demo_label` (about 300 accounts, `AC-101`..`AC-400`) |
| `demo_transactions.csv` | `transaction_id, source_account, destination_account, amount, currency, timestamp, channel, device_id, description` (about 1,600 transfers, 1 Aug to 30 Sep 2026, INR) |
| `labels.csv` | `transaction_id, scenario_id, label`: ground truth for evaluation only. The risk engine and Gemini never read it |

Regenerate with `python scripts/generate_dataset.py`. The output is byte-identical every time.

## Background traffic (label LEGIT)
- Monthly salaries from 12 employers to 100 employees (1 Aug and 1 Sep).
- Monthly utility and bill payments to merchants.
- 550 peer transfers, mostly within a fixed friend circle, with log-normal amounts.
- 300 card purchases.
- About 90% of personal payments carry a per-account device ID. The rest have no device ID, which tests missing context.

## Labeled scenarios
| Scenario | Label | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| `layered-transfer-01` | SUSPICIOUS | One account sends funds to 5 new accounts (sharing device `DEV-6602`). Each forwards the funds within 7-12 minutes to one of 2 intermediaries, and both intermediaries consolidate into a collector (12 transfers). One description contains a prompt-injection string to test that it is treated as data. |
| `rapid-onward-01` | SUSPICIOUS | 180k forwarded twice within 16 minutes. |
| `fan-in-out-01` | SUSPICIOUS | 6 transfers of about 9k from different senders, followed 28 minutes after the first by a 56k transfer out. |
| `legit-high-value-01` | LEGIT | 2.5M property payment to a builder, which pays a supplier 1.1M 22 minutes later. This is a lookalike. |
| `shared-device-01` | LEGIT | 4 family accounts share `DEV-7781` for small payments, plus one larger tuition payment to a new payee. |
| `missing-baseline-01` | LEGIT | A new account's first transfer, with no history and no device. |
| `repeated-payments-01` | LEGIT | 9 weekly gym payments. |

## Known limitations
- The same team designed both the scenarios and the rules, so detection is easier than it would be on real data.
- The history window is two months, so "new relationship" and baselines are approximate.
- Amounts and timings are illustrative and not calibrated to any real institution.
