## LinkedIn post

We built FraudGraph AI for the Google AI Builder Cup 2026.

Fraud analysts get alerts one transfer at a time, but fraud moves across many accounts in minutes. FraudGraph AI links those transfers into a single case and draws the money flow, so the pattern is visible at a glance.

What we're proud of:
- Gemini explains each case using only that case's evidence, and every citation is verified before it is shown.
- Upload a receipt or invoice and Gemini reads it, then fixed rules check it against the transfer.
- Mitigation actions are recommended by rules and by AI, but nothing happens until a person approves it. An approved hold then stops new transfers on that account until a person releases or rejects them.
- Everything is logged: who decided what, and why.

It runs on synthetic data only. No real customer information is used.
