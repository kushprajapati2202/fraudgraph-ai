FC-02 Holds, monitoring and transfer controls

Illustrative internal policy written for the FraudGraph AI sandbox. Not legal or regulatory guidance.

1. Hold for review

1.1 A hold stops new transfers to or from the account until an analyst releases or rejects each one.

1.2 A hold requires an approving analyst, a linked case and a stated reason. Holds are never applied automatically by a model.

1.3 A hold must be reviewed within 48 hours. If no new evidence supports it, the hold is lifted with a reason recorded in the audit history.

2. Enhanced monitoring

2.1 Enhanced monitoring flags every new transfer on the account for 30 days regardless of score.

2.2 Use enhanced monitoring instead of a hold when the evidence is MEDIUM priority or when the customer has provided a plausible explanation that is not yet verified.

3. Proportionality

Controls must be proportionate. Legitimate customers waiting on held payments should be contacted promptly, and a matching supporting document (amount, date, parties and bank reference) is a sufficient reason to release a held transfer.
