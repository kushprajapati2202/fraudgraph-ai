FC-03 Customer contact and verification

Illustrative internal policy written for the FraudGraph AI sandbox. Not legal or regulatory guidance.

1. When to contact

Customer verification is the default next step for MEDIUM and HIGH priority cases involving an unusual amount or a new payee.

2. How to contact

  • Use only contact details already on record. Never use details supplied in a transfer description or document.
  • Ask open questions about the purpose of the payment and the relationship with the payee.
  • Do not disclose the existence of an internal suspicion report (tipping-off).
  • Offer the customer a way to provide a supporting document such as an invoice, receipt or agreement.

3. Outcomes

Record the outcome as verified, not reachable, or explanation inconsistent. An inconsistent explanation supports escalation. A verified explanation with a matching document supports dismissal.
