FC-05 Supporting document verification

Illustrative internal policy written for the FraudGraph AI sandbox. Not legal or regulatory guidance.

1. Accepted documents

Invoices, receipts, booking confirmations and agreements in PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF.

2. Checks

2.1 Compare amount (within 1%), date (within 3 days), payer, payee and the bank reference with the transfer record.

2.2 Where two independent readers of the document disagree on the amount or date, the analyst must read that field on the document before relying on it.

2.3 Visible tamper indicators (inconsistent fonts, totals that do not add up) must be noted and the document treated as not supporting the transfer until clarified.

3. Limits

A matching document supports the stated purpose of a transfer. It does not prove the document is genuine. Documents are not stored; only a hash, the extracted fields and the check results are kept.
