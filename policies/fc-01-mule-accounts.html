FC-01 Money mule and pass-through accounts

Illustrative internal policy written for the FraudGraph AI sandbox. Not legal or regulatory guidance.

1. Purpose

This policy sets out how analysts identify and respond to accounts that receive funds and move them onward quickly (pass-through or mule accounts).

2. Indicators

  • At least 70% of an inbound amount moved onward within 30 minutes.
  • Funds received from several unrelated senders and consolidated into one outbound transfer (many-in, rapid-out).
  • Recently opened accounts or minimum-KYC accounts acting as intermediaries.
  • A device shared by three or more customer accounts.

3. Required response

3.1 A HIGH priority case showing rapid onward movement must be reviewed by an analyst the same business day.

3.2 Where funds remain in the intermediary account, the analyst may approve a Hold for review on that account. A hold must be reviewed within 48 hours and each held transfer released or rejected with a written reason.

3.3 Customer verification should be attempted before an account is escalated, unless contacting the customer could alert a suspected organiser.

3.4 Where the pattern persists after verification, the analyst escalates the case and drafts an internal suspicion report.

4. What analysts must not do

Analysts must not state that a customer is guilty. Findings are described as patterns consistent with mule activity. Protected characteristics must never be used as a reason for action.
