FC-04 Escalation and suspicion reporting

Illustrative internal policy written for the FraudGraph AI sandbox. Not legal or regulatory guidance.

1. Escalation criteria

Escalate a case when any of the following holds after review: the pattern persists after customer contact; the customer explanation is inconsistent with the evidence; a supporting document does not support the transfer; or the case links to an account already under a hold.

2. Internal suspicion report

2.1 An escalated case requires an internal suspicion report drafted within 2 business days.

2.2 The report must cite the evidence on which it relies, separate observed facts from inferences, state alternative legitimate explanations and record uncertainty.

2.3 AI-drafted text is advisory. The analyst reviews, edits and approves the report before it is submitted to the financial-crime team.

3. Dismissal

A case may be dismissed with a written rationale when the activity is explained and documented. The rationale and evidence remain in the audit history.
